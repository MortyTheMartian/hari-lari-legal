Hari Lari Reports

Hari Lari Reports is the internal reporting tool of Hari Lari, a café in Bali, Indonesia. Every Monday it builds the weekly sales report from the café's own point-of-sale data and shares it with the owners and managers of Hari Lari through a private Telegram group and a private Google Drive folder.

The tool is used only by the owner of Hari Lari. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
