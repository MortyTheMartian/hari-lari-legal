Privacy policy of Hari Lari Reports

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

What the app is

Hari Lari Reports is the internal reporting tool of Hari Lari (home). It is used by one person, the owner of Hari Lari, who gives it access to his own Google Drive.

What it does with your Google account

After the owner gives consent once, the app uploads the weekly sales report (a PDF file and an Excel file) into one Google Drive folder of the owner, and creates sub-folders in that folder. It does nothing else in Google Drive. The folder is shared only with named owners and managers of Hari Lari.

What data it handles

The reports contain the café's sales figures (items, quantities, amounts, times of sale). They contain no names or contact details of customers. The app does not collect any data from other users or from the public.

Sharing, storage and retention

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising. The access token that the owner's consent produces is kept in Google Secret Manager, in the project of Hari Lari, and is used only by the weekly report job. The owner can withdraw the consent at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the uploads then stop.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
